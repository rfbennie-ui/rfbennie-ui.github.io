Privacy policy

This application is used only by its owner to back up the owner's own files to the owner's own Google Drive.

  • It accesses Google Drive files only in the owner's account, and only to store and manage backup copies.
  • It collects no personal data from anyone else.
  • No data is shared with, sold to or transferred to any third party.
  • Access can be removed at any time in the Google Account settings under third-party access.
